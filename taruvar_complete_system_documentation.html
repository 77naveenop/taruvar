1. Executive Summary & Core Mission

TARUVAR ("One Person. One Tree. / एक व्यक्ति, एक पेड़") is a technology-enabled grassroots environmental organization and public movement built on the core belief that planting a tree is only the beginning.

Traditional environmental drives focus solely on the gross number of saplings planted during one-day events, resulting in over 80% sapling mortality due to lack of ongoing care. Taruvar changes this through individual guardianship (Paalna / देखभाल), requiring ongoing nurturing, photographic documentation, and a mandatory 5-month growth verification cycle.

The 5-Step Taruvar Framework

  1. 1. Plant (पौधा लगाना): Individual plants a climate-appropriate sapling, selects species, and uploads photo proof.
  2. 2. Care (देखभाल / Paalna): Guardian provides water, nutrients, and protection throughout seasonal heat and winter.
  3. 3. Document (तस्वीर व लॉग): Adopter uploads monthly growth progress photos and observations.
  4. 4. Grow (निरंतर विकास): Tree progresses through Month 1 to Month 5 verification milestones.
  5. 5. Inspire (प्रेरणा): Verified trees earn the official Level 5 Eco-Guardian Badge and rank on the community feed.

4.3 Conflict-Resilient Retry Mechanism

The client employs putGithubJsonWithRetry() featuring 5-stage exponential backoff. Whenever multiple guardians submit requests simultaneously, the client fetches the fresh SHA hash before committing to guarantee zero merge conflicts and complete data integrity.

8. Step-by-Step Operational Workflows

8.1 Tree Adoption to Verified Badge Lifecycle

  1. Step 1 (Adoption): User navigates to /adopt, chooses species, enters nickname/location, and uploads a mandatory plantation photo.
  2. Step 2 (ID Generation): System immediately generates Member ID (TRV-IND-2026-XXXX) and Tree Passport ID (TRV-TREE-XXXX), rendering the printable PVC ID card.
  3. Step 3 (Admin Review): Admin opens Admin Desk from the top navbar, reviews the photo proof, and approves the adoption.
  4. Step 4 (5-Month Progress Logs): Every 30 days, the user uploads Month 1 to 5 growth photos in their Profile.
  5. Step 5 (Verification & Badges): Admin verifies monthly progress logs, advancing the user's status to Level 5 Verified Eco-Guardian.
